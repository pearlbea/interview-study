# Interview Study

Flashcards for preparing for technical interviews. Learners work through Cards grouped by Topic and rate how well they recalled each Answer.

## Language

### Cards

**Card**:
A single study item, made up of a Question on the front and an Answer on the back.
_Avoid_: Question (for the whole item), flashcard

**Question**:
The prompt on the front of a Card.
_Avoid_: Prompt

**Answer**:
The reference answer on the back of a Card, written by an admin.
_Avoid_: Our answer, solution

**Topic**:
A subject area that groups Cards, such as React or Postgres. A Card belongs to one or more Topics.
_Avoid_: Category, tag

### Studying

**Quiz**:
A collection of Cards that a learner flips through one at a time.
_Avoid_: Session, deck

**Your Answer**:
The text a learner writes in response to a Question before flipping the Card. It lasts only while that Card is showing, so the learner can compare it with the Answer, and is discarded when the next Card loads.
_Avoid_: Answer (on its own), attempt, response

**Review**:
The record of one learner rating one Card at one moment. A learner builds up many Reviews of the same Card over time.
_Avoid_: Rating event, attempt

**Rating**:
The learner's judgment, given as part of a Review, of how well they recalled the Answer: Not at all, Partially, Pretty much, or Easy peasy.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, score, Again/Hard/Good/Easy
